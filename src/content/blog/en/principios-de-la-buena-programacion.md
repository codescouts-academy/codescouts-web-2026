### Good Programming Principles

After years of experience, the industry has coined many acronyms that refer to best practices, and we often reference them.

It is important to know them so we can leverage expert knowledge, have more efficient discussions, and evaluate our work more critically.

### KISS: Keep it simple, stupid!, but I prefer Keep it stupidly simple!

![Untitled](/images/blog/kiss.png)

> "Code is easier to maintain and extend if we keep it simple and do not add more complexity than the problem inherently requires."

### YAGNI: You ain’t gonna need it!

![Untitled](/images/blog/yagni.png)

> "Do not implement something until it is strictly necessary."

### Baby steps - Simplest thing

![Untitled](/images/blog/simplest-thing.png)

> "Progress toward solving a problem is maximized when we work in small, simple steps oriented toward the solution."

### Separation of concerns

![Untitled](/images/blog/separation-of-concerns.png)

> "It is a design principle in which we divide our application into different sections, so each section is responsible for one concern. For example, business logic, presentation logic, and persistence are different concerns."

### DRY: Don't repeat yourself

![Untitled](/images/blog/dry.png)

> "Each piece of knowledge should have a single, unambiguous, canonical representation in the system."

### Code for the maintainer

![Untitled](/images/blog/code-of-the-mainteiner.png)

"Always program as if the person who will maintain your code is a psychopath who knows where you live."

## Avoid premature optimization

![Untitled](/images/blog/avoid-premature-optimization.png)

> "Premature optimization is the act of trying to make things more efficient at a stage when it is too early to do so."

## Boy-Scout rule

![Untitled](/images/blog/boy-scout-rule.png)

> "Leave things BETTER than you found them." ~Robert Baden Powell

### Make it work, make it right, make it fast

![Untitled](/images/blog/doit-meme.png)

> "Focus your solution on solving the problem at hand without spending more time than necessary. Once the problem is solved, improve the solution and make it faster."
